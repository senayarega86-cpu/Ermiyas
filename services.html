Our services

Services that help Ethiopian businesses compete and grow

From the policy table to global markets, ECCSA offers 16 services across advocacy, trade & investment, and business development.

Advocacy & Policy Trade & Investment Business Development Leadership Academy Trade Facilitation Feedback
01 · Advocacy & policy

A strong voice in policy-making

We represent the private sector in policy dialogue, research and public-private engagement to drive economic growth.

01

Business Advocacy

Representing businesses in policy dialogues and advocating for a conducive business environment for business owners, investors and entrepreneurs.

02

Policy Research & Analysis

Conducting studies on economic policies and business challenges to inform evidence-based dialogue.

03

Public-Private Dialogue

Bridging the gap between the government and the private sector.

04

B2G Engagement

Facilitating dialogue between businesses and government bodies.

02 · Trade & investment

Connecting Ethiopian business to the world

We facilitate trade fairs, investment forums and global market linkages that help businesses grow, including the Ethio-Chamber International Trade Fair.

05

Trade Promotion

Facilitating trade fairs, B2B meetings and international market linkages for exporters, importers, manufacturers and service providers.

06

Investment Promotion

Promoting investment opportunities for domestic and foreign investors and linking investors with local businesses.

07

Export Facilitation

Supporting businesses to enter international markets.

08

Import Facilitation

Helping businesses source raw materials and products.

09

Business Certification

Issuing trade-related certificates for credibility and compliance.

10

Business Information Services

Providing market intelligence and business data.

11

Business Networking

Creating platforms for businesses to connect and collaborate.

12

Sector-Specific Support

Specialized support for industries such as agriculture, manufacturing and services.

03 · Business development

Building stronger, more competitive enterprises

We provide training, consultancy and dispute resolution to strengthen business competitiveness.

13

Capacity Building Training

Training programs in business management, leadership and trade regulation.

14

Business Advisory Services

Guidance on business expansion, strategy and legal compliance.

15

SME Development Support

Helping small businesses grow through financial and technical assistance.

16

Arbitration & Dispute Resolution

Mediating business conflicts to avoid lengthy court cases.

Business Leadership Academy

Developing the next generation of business leaders

The ECCSA Business Leadership Academy delivers practical training for chamber leaders, managers and entrepreneurs, backed by a network of more than 250 certified trainers.

  • Leadership & governance
  • Business management
  • Trade regulations
  • Strategic planning
