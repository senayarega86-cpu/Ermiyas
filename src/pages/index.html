About ECCSA

A catalyst for business success

The Ethiopian Chamber of Commerce and Sectoral Associations is a 75-year-old institution representing the private sector nationwide. We are the bridge between businesses and government.

Committed to empowering Ethiopian enterprises, ECCSA enhances business competitiveness, expands market access, influences economic policy and builds strategic alliances for national and global success.

  • Policy advocacy & public-private dialogue
  • Trade fairs & market linkages
  • Capacity building & training
  • Arbitration & dispute resolution
