Who we are

Bridging business and government

The Ethiopian Chamber of Commerce and Sectoral Associations (ECCSA) is the apex body representing Ethiopia's private sector nationwide.

ECCSA serves as the bridge between businesses and the government, advocating for policy reform, promoting trade and supporting economic development. Through our member regional chambers, city chambers and sectoral associations, we reach businesses of every size in every sector.

We provide services including business networking, capacity building, market linkages and dispute resolution, fostering a vibrant and inclusive business ecosystem.

Our history

Tracing our legacy from 1947 to today's economy

Over the decades, ECCSA has evolved in response to Ethiopia's changing economic policies.

1947

Establishment

Ethiopia's first chamber of commerce was established under General Notice No. 90/1947, with voluntary membership.

1971

Reorganized with branches

Branches opened in key cities such as Asmara, Dire Dawa, Jimma, Gonder and Adama.

1974

Restructured

Restructured under Proclamation No. 148/1974, making membership mandatory under the centrally planned economy.

2003

Re-established

Proclamation No. 341/2003 re-established the chamber system for Ethiopia's free-market economy, creating ECCSA in its current form.
